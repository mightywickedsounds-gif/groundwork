# Flowtown (repo: groundwork)

A browser traffic game, hosted on GitHub Pages from `main` (root): https://mightywickedsounds-gif.github.io/groundwork/. This is a standalone project. The repo is public: never mention or link the owner's other projects, and keep personal details out of files and commit messages.

- No build step. three.js 0.160 from jsDelivr via the import map in `index.html` (CSP meta there; add any new host). The import map also pins our own modules to `?v=N` (and `game.js?v=N` in the script tag): bump N in both places on every change that reaches `main`, or phones mix cached old and new files (the title screen's `.ver` label shows the loaded game.js `?v`).
- `net.js` (no three, runs in Node): `Net` = nodes/edges (quadratic curves `cx,cz`, `oneway` 0/1/-1, `ring` id, `kind`), `probe`/`realize`/`planRoad`/`addRoad` (plan first without changing anything; every crossing becomes a junction or the road is refused with a `.reason`; `crossesOthers` + `restore(snapshot)` undo anything that would leave a crossing without a junction; snap radius passed in from the zoom), `removeEdge`, `toggleSignal`, `cycleOneWay`, `makeRoundabout`/`roundaboutOnRoad`/`removeRoundabout` (radius from `ringFit` toward `ringWant(spokes)` = room for every spoke's width, up to 30, min 8; `ringLanes` = 2 ring lanes when a wide road comes in, ring edges carry `nl`; on a ring approach every lane serves every movement; stored per ring node as `rc` [x,z,R]; close spokes share an entrance; one-way arcs going toward decreasing angle). Lanes: each edge has `nl` lanes each way (1-3) and `tl` turn lanes (`LANE_STEPS`, `cycleLanes` = Lanes tool over `roadChain` through bends, adds lights where 3+ wide legs meet); width from `halfW(e)`, lane offset `laneOff(e, i)` (i = nl is the turn lane, in the middle for two-way). `rebuild()` makes lanes (right-hand traffic, right normal is (-dz, dx), x east, z south; key `<edge>f|b` = kerb lane, `f1`.. inner, `fp` turn lane; `li`, `pk`, `off`, `Fa/Fb` for `mapS`, `chg` = lanes it may move to, `mv` arrow bits 1 straight 2 right 4 left) trimmed by `trimOf(node)` (grows with width), turn lanes where a left turn exists (`e.pk` = lengths at ends a/b), and connectors per node from lane assignment (kerb lane right, inner lane left, weights straight 3) via `connect()` with `conflicts`; keeps cars and their routes when lanes survive. `step(dt)`: lane changes (`chgTo` = next route lane beside, `gapOK`, wait near the end, cars behind yield via `mq`, sideways drift `lat`), IDM following, junction permission `canEnter` (room on the out-lane, conflicting connectors `busy`, signal state, ring priority + ring fill cap `ringOcc`, first-come-first-served `arrT`), give up after `GIVE_UP` s; a granted car that can't move lets go after 2 s. Drawing pose: front wheels at `posAt(car, s - .2 len)` + `lat`, rear wheels `rx/rz` dragged behind at `.6 len` (no slides or snaps; set `rx/rz` when handing a car over); `lat`/`latV`/`latT` = sideways offset eased by a spring capped by speed (lane change drift, pulling out from / in to the kerb); a car mid-change is still in its old lane's `vis` list (cars there keep behind it); `sigT` = blinker on ~1 s before moving over; `sig` = blinker 0/1 left/2 right. Routing = Dijkstra on lanes with `cost` (live lane speed `spd`) plus lane moves `chgCost`; trips start/end in kerb lanes. Saves: edge arrays end with `nl, tl, lv`. Over/underpasses: edge `lv` 1/-1, height `edgeH(e, s)` = `profileH` (level `FLAT` m by each node, ramps `rampLen` at `GRADE` with `VCURVE` rounding, `H_OVER`/`H_UNDER`); crossings without a junction are allowed only where `clears()` finds `CLEARANCE` all the way across (`sepAt` in `crossesOthers`; `planRoad(..., lv)` bridges every crossing or refuses with a reason; `probe` never joins an lv edge mid-way); cars get `y`/`pitch` from `laneH`/`hAt`; lv edges carry no buildings, sidewalks, walkers, parking, scenery or hiding units. `MAP_EDGE` = no roads beyond 1600 m (hills). Cars: `temper` speed factor from the colour roll, `TYPES[].acc` slower trucks/buses, `stopAt(n, e)` = stop sign (slow to `STOP_V` at the line; avenues/wide roads don't stop for smaller streets). A trip ends only once the car has stopped (`v < 1.2`; lot cars hand over on the move); `kerbLat(lane, w)` = sideways move to pull in by the curb (30 cm off it); `retarget(car, at)` = new trip end in the kerb lane by `at` (a lot that filled up: `net.lotFull(car)`, set by life, checked 70 m out; `car.kerbAt`). `net.peds` = people out in the road (set by life each step): cars stop for them. `lotSpots` near a junction (trim + 45 m) = right turns only. Dead-end U-turn connectors `vmax` 2.2.
- `layout.js` (no three): plan 2 street plan, all from hashes of the seed: `makePlan(seed)` -> `highway` (Main St straight `A`-`B` in town, smooth chains out to `REACH`), `ranch` (crosses Main at a slant), `village` (old-town streets), `side(k, ci, cj)` (district side: jittered corners, C/S bow, `on` = avenue else street, `t` = where streets meet it, `at(s)` by arc length), `district(i, j, kind?, rot?)` (arterials + streets drawn in a Coons patch `Q(u,v)` of the bent outline: collector + side streets/cul-de-sacs, loop, collector + crescent; `trim` = cut where it first meets a road). Roads are control polygons -> `pieces()` (quadratics between side midpoints) -> `sample()`.
- `city.js` (no three): `C.plan` 1 = old grid (saves without `plan`), 2 = layout.js (new towns): `base()` (highway, ranch, village; ranch gets a centre turn lane in the old town; `lights()` where 3+ avenue legs meet), `unlock2` (arterials unless `covered`, streets only where `fits()` (no crowding alongside, shallow crossings, bunched junctions; ends snap onto a junction within 24 m), all-or-nothing per street, must join a road; tries other patterns if most fail; removes anything left unconnected), `fillDistrict` (non-bare unlock), `C.names`/`nameOf(e)` (street names, rebuilt on load). Plan 1: `BLOCK` grid, districts of `CHUNK` blocks unlocked centre-out (`order`, `nextDistrict`, `unlock`; district-edge avenues = arterials built as 2 lanes + turn lane with lights where 3+ avenue legs meet), `fillBlock` per block side: `core` (town centre within `CORE_R`), `corridor` (avenue frontage: shop strips / big stores / offices / garden apartments with parking lots), `home` (houses with `lw`/`ld` lot size for fences); `SETBACK`/`SETBACK_AV`; `TRIPS` per resident; parks/parking/yards (a lot may have `rot` and `bid`: `frontLot(b)` puts one between a business/apartment and its street, `ensureLots()` for old saves, drawn in its own frame), `sync()` clears buildings a road runs through (never shrinks them; lots may be cut back via `cutsFor`/`cutBack`), `maxHalfW(chain)` = widest carriageway before a building (house 3 m past the sidewalk, others .8, business lots .3) is in the way (the Lanes tool passes it to `nextLanes`/`cycleLanes(eid, maxHW)`, which skip steps that don't fit), `inTheWay(pts, hw)` = buildings a planned road would clear, and attaches buildings to streets; `regrow(dt)` (game + grow test) adds buildings along any street inside unlocked districts facing it (`b.rot`: local x axis = (cos, sin); drawn with rotation.y = -rot; saved as building field 15), committing every 12 s (`C.ver++`); `hitsRoad` pads wide roads by `halfW - 3.5`, `demand`/`release` trips by `rushCurve(hour)`, `wild()` woods on unbuilt land (looks only). Never add random draws to existing generation (it would move things in saved cities); hash/seed only.
- `sky.js`: `makeSky(renderer)` sky dome shader (time of day, sunset, clouds, stars, moon), `horizon()` = fog colour, `env()` re-renders the environment map (PMREM) from the same sky when the sun moves. `game.js` `lighting(hour)` drives it (sun east -> south at noon -> west), `hillH` = hills beyond `HILL0` (ground mesh displaced; camera kept above them).
- `draw.js`: `buildRoads` (`gradeSep` draws lv edges: normal `piece`s by the junctions, a `bare` piece lifted onto `edgeH`, concrete `ST` (barriers, embankment walls or cutting walls + parapet, deck + beams over spans, tunnel roof, abutment/portal faces; `concreteMaterial`), `MASK` = stencil marks where the ground (stencil NotEqual in game.js) is cut over open cuttings; sidewalks use `stripUV` + `walkTex` panels, `M.slab` for world-space concrete; `piece` = one stretch of road; two roads joined end to end are drawn as one smooth `elbow` (control = where the end headings meet), not a junction; `piece(..., ends)` sets exact end normals so pieces meet without slivers; asphalt strips, junction fans with curb returns `corner` (straight kerb when legs are ~opposite; sidewalk outer edge = kerb offset), ring markings as full circles, raised sidewalks, markings (`seg`, `hatch` median, per-lane `turnArrow` from `mv`), stop/give-way lines, zebras, islands, street-light spots), `buildCity` (instanced buildings with the window shader `buildingMaterial`: photo walls by `aWall` 0 stucco / 1 brick / 2 limestone from `FACADE`, hash inputs quantised; `roofMaterial` shingles in world space; parapets, roof units, awnings, garage/front doors, chimneys, shrubs, lawns, patios, pools; lots, parked cars via `staticCarGeo`), trees = leaf cards (`cards()`: clusters from `leafTex` facing out of each lobe, crown-centre normals, dark solid `lobes` core using the texture's corner `CORE_UV`; `oakGeo`/`cedarGeo`, `lite` for the wild woods; `t[3] < .7` = oak) with `leafMaterial` (alphaTest + mip coverage fix, `userData.depth` for dappled shadows, wind sway via `U.uTime`); lawns use `M.tex.grass`, photo textures from `assets/tex/` (`makeMaterials`, `patchy` breaks up repeats), vehicles (`vehicleGeos`: extruded side profiles + narrower glass `cabin`; looks car/hatch/suv/pickup/van/truck/bus, `LOOK` in game.js maps sim types to looks; `aLight` 1 head / 2 tail / 3-4 left/right blinker, per-instance `aBrake` = brake + 2 * blinker), headlight `beamMaterial`, people (`personGeo` parts + `personMaterial`: limbs swing by per-instance `aWalk`, `aLook` = skin/trousers/hair), lamp models, `buildSignals` (mast arms with a head per lane, lamp list for `drawSignals`; stop signs on plain junctions), `CLOUD` / `cloudUniforms` = drifting cloud shadows (`U.uCloudSh`, `U.uCover`) shared by ground, roads, buildings, roofs, leaves. Weather: `lighting()` picks a cloud cover per day (hash of day + seed, blended) for the sky (`sky.U.uCloud`), cloud shadows and sun strength. Night uses `U.uNight`.
- `game.js`: camera (`cam` target/dist/yaw/pitch), tools (`TOOLS`, `click`, `hoverUpdate`, `previewRoad`, `place`, chain pin `T.pts`, curve pending `T.pend`/`T.bend`, `snapR()` = tap radius from zoom; touch: a quick drag always pans; hold `HOLD` ms then drag draws with Road/Curve (`startDraw`; decided by touch `timeStamp`s at the first real move, the timer only shows the sparkle), two fingers turn/tilt/zoom). `probe` joins any road whose sidewalk the point is on (reach `halfW + 2*WALK_W + 5`), magic sparkles (`burst`), traffic view (`buildOverlay`/`paintOverlay`, one band per edge direction), jam markers, day/night `lighting(hour)` (night is moonlit blue, `dark` = 0.6 of `night`; `nightOn` menu switch; `clockRate` 3x from 21:30 to 5:00), gridlock meter (`simStep`), growth (`growInterval`), saving (`flowtown.save.v1`, every 10 s + pagehide), undo (`snapTown`/`undoStack`: net + city + staff + coins before each road edit, `preEdit` pushed by `afterEdit`; `#undoBtn` for 8 s, Ctrl+Z), lamp glows (`carFlares`: brake/blinker sprites), quality `flowtown.quality` (`QS`), sound (made in code). `window.FT` = test hooks.
- `life.js` (no three): people derived from homes (`pid = building id * 256 + k`, names/age/insurance/tags/temper by hash), jobs (`biz.staff` order = rank: owner, manager, staff; `title`, `boss`, `shift`), routines in `decide` (commute, lunch walk, errands, evening strolls; `go` walks under ~140 m else queues a car trip with `ok`/`fail` callbacks; `carDone` = driver walks from the car to the door: `carWalk` (far side of the street: to that sidewalk and across properly)). Walks: `walkRoute` crosses at junctions on the zebras (`zebraT` = trim - 2.1, `roundCorner` = fewest crossings round the junction), a busy road (avenue, 2+ lanes, turn lane) only at a junction; `crossings(w)` = stretches in the roadway (`w.xs`): wait at the curb until `roadClear` (15/30 s at most), then `net.peds`; `lotCarAhead` = wait for a moving lot car, `aroundCar` round a stopped one (3 times at most); visitors (`guests`, through traffic from `exits()` = dead ends outside town), businesses `BIZ` (levels 1-5, `rate`, `spend`, `hours`, `titles`, `pop` gate; till `cash` up to `cashCap`, manager banks from level 3), coins, `COST`, `FINE`, goals, day ledger (`events` 'day'/'goal'). Saves: `life.serialize()` = coins, goals, staff lists; buildings save `biz` as field 18.
- `police.js` (no three): units from stations (`syncUnits`), hiding spots (`findSpots`), radar in `hourly`, `pursue`/`chaseStep` (traffic stop -> ticket, or flee -> arrest/escape), `crash` (cars `hold`+`crashed`, uninsured may flee), `dispatch` to incidents, robberies/carjackings, road rage; `feed` = scanner. net.js car flags: `hold` (stopped in place), `aside` (pulled over: others pass), `siren`/`flee` (through reds), `reckless` (speed, tailgate, may run a red: `ranRed`), `keep` (units: not removed at the destination), `yieldT` (pulling over for a siren). `SVC_TYPES` police/ems/fire via `spawn(..., force)`.
- `parking.js` (no three): lot `frame`/`layout` (rows, stalls, aisles; islands at row ends; a lot with `fuel` (gas station, set in `frontLot`/`load`) keeps one stall row and leaves `pumpZone(l)` for the canopy + pump islands drawn in draw.js) and `entries` (driveways to the nearest street), shared by draw.js and life.js; `createParking` = `life.park`: `arrive` (car drives in to a free stall, driver walks in), `leave` (driver walks out, car hidden+held in the lane while the stand-in backs out, stops at the bar, then hands over), `parked` (drawn by game.js with the fleet), `parkNow` for workers after loading. Only lots with `bid` whose driveway is on the building's own street are driven into. `hasFree(b)`; `footPath(b, door)` = on foot from the door along the shopfront, between two stalls of the first row (the accessible stall's striped aisle if any), behind the tails to the driveway, beside it to the sidewalk (`L.door(b).path`); `aisleZ` keeps aisle driving lines clear of the parked cars' tails; parking swings in from the far side of the aisle (`mouth` 3.4 m out), backing out goes on straight until square; a leaving car's stall is `moving` (drawn as the mover only); movers stop for `P.peds` right in front. Rows past the first leave a drive-through gap in line with each driveway. Movers drive `leg`s (cubic `curve`s between poses, speed from the bend, `frontBack` = guide wheels + trailing wheels); trips to/from a lot use `net.lotSpots` (lane on the lot side, or the inside lane across the road); arriving car leaves a `net.block` in its lane while turning; `clear()` waits for a gap (patience after 25/45 s, a waiting car keeps its place after 6 s); `walkPath(k, door)` = the driver's walk between the stalls to the door.
- Roads (round 18): `GU` concrete gutters + `CT` curb tops along every curb (straight, corners, dead ends), `parkway(e)` = grass strip by the curb on house streets (`PKW`; walkers use offsets 1.75-2.7 so they stay on the concrete), US paint (no lines on `street`, yellow centre on other two-way roads, edge lines only on bigger roads), `TP` tactile pads where crosswalks meet corners, storm grates + manholes (`plate`), `opt.rural(e)` = country highway outside town (shoulders `SH`, gravel `GR`, no curb/sidewalk/lights). Asphalt shader adds crack sealant and patches. `driveRects` (scenery.js) keeps street lights (`buildLamps`), furniture and trees off lot and house driveways.
- `scenery.js`: shop name signs (canvas atlas), street trees (passed to buildCity as `opt.more`), poles + wires, hydrants, blades, speed signs, bus shelters, benches, bins, grass/flower clumps. Rebuilt with the city.
- Phones (max-width 760): `#spdCol` = pause/play, speed (1x/2x/4x round) and traffic view tiles under the menu; `#bar` holds 8 tools. Game flow: boot screen -> title (`titleUp`, orbit camera) -> `play()`; `flowtown.save.v2`; no game over (gridlock just costs income); `onTapWorld` picks coins, people, cars, hidden units, buildings -> `#card` (`.mini` while following: `setFollow`, chase camera in `selTick`, one-finger drag orbits); `law.report(pid)` = lookout (`bolo`), next drive a unit pursues; phone HUD collapses (`#hud.open`); Road/Curve show `#lvl` (Street/Overpass/Underpass = `T.lv`, `setLv`); Build tool -> `#pick`; day summary `#daySum` pauses.

Run them after any traffic or tool change and report results honestly.

It's an original game in the traffic/city genre: don't copy any existing game's name, art, levels or look.

Screen style (owner): solid road-sign UI (round 2): green guide sign `#hud`, yellow warning sign `#jam`, asphalt dock `#bar` with yellow for the active tile, `.sign` panels (inset border line), Overpass font in `assets/fonts/`. The owner hates the see-through/glassy floating-text look (it reads as AI-made): no translucent panels, text shadows, glows or letter-spaced captions in the UI. Keep it minimal: short labels, no paragraphs on screen, no emoji. Look: semi-realistic aerial view of a Central Texas Hill Country town (real lighting and shadows): 1-2 storey houses with hip roofs, brick/limestone/stucco, driveways and wooden fences; strip centres, big stores, offices and garden apartments on arterials (4 lanes + two-way left-turn lane); a downtown that grows to 3-7 storeys once the town is bigger (owner asked for taller and denser); live oaks and cedars. Don't name the real town in files. Simplified driving.
