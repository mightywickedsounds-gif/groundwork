# Groundwork

A browser digging game, hosted on GitHub Pages from `main` (root): https://mightywickedsounds-gif.github.io/groundwork/. This is a standalone project. The repo is public: never mention or link the owner's other projects, and keep personal details out of files and commit messages.

- `index.html`: page, HUD, menus, touch controls. Has a CSP meta; add any new host there.
- `game.js`: the whole game, an ES module. three.js 0.160 from jsDelivr via the import map. No build step.
  - World v2 (round 4): 256x256 m, `RIVERS` (control points with bed levels, `riverAt` also beyond the map), `SPRINGS`, marsh berm, `GRAVEL`/`YARD`/`CAMP`/`MARSH`, `GOALS` (cross/haul/dam/camp/marsh) + side jobs, `SPEC[].ford` (machines refuse water deeper than this), minimap. Save key `groundwork.save.v3` (older ones left untouched). River profile is `carve()` (valley 3x bank wide, floodplain = water level + .6, channel 1.7x half-width); used by generation and the outer land (`farH`). Glass and other see-through materials must not write depth. Sparse sim: `waterActive` 16 m tiles (`scanWater`), `tileLoose` for `relax`; always call `dirty(k)` after changing `H`.
  - Drawing: terrain in 32 m chunks (`tchunks`, `updateTerrainChunks`), detail 2 (0.5 m, Catmull-Rom `hSmooth` + soil `lump`) near the camera, skirts hide seams; water per chunk; card trees (`spruceGeo`/`leafyGeo`, foliage atlas in textures.js); sounds are generated samples (`engineSample`, `pourSample`, `streamSample` in audio.js).
  - Height grid `H` (1 m cells) with `BED` dig floor and `M` material (0 grass, 1 dirt, 2 gravel, `D` disturbed).
  - Dirt sliding: `relax` (talus .92 dry, .62 wet).
  - Water: virtual-pipes `waterStep`, spring on the west edge (`SPRING_Q`), drain on the east edge.
  - Water drawing (round 17): each chunk's water is on the same grid as its ground (`fillWater` uses the chunk's drawn heights `c.hh`, `c.hhF`), so `aW` is depth over the drawn ground and the shoreline is where they meet (fragment discards `vW <= 0`). `ripple(x, z, s)` adds a ring (`ripU`, 16 slots: dirt dropped in, machine wakes). `REFL` (High, Medium on computers) renders a half/third-size mirror image from a camera reflected about the level of the water in view (oblique near plane, as three's Reflector) into `tRefl`; water far from that level fades back to the painted sky and tree line. Ground under shallow water gets caustics (terrain shader `uTime`); `cellLook` turns grass within 2 m of water into wet mud and pebbles (looks only). `GW.camFix([x,y,z,lx,ly,lz])` fixes the camera for screenshots.
  - Machines: `SPEC`/`BUILD`/`TOOL` for dozer, loader, excavator, hauler. Loads are `{v, g}` (volume, gravel volume).
  - Jobs: `JOB` (level / pond / stock).
  - Saving: localStorage `groundwork.save.v3`, every 5 s and on pagehide. Never reset or wipe a save except through the confirmed "New world" in Menu → Worlds, which first keeps the current valley as "Autosave" in IndexedDB (`groundwork` db, `worlds` store, `saveData()` JSON).
  - `window.GW.step(n, input)` runs the game without drawing; tests use it.
- `models.js`: machine builders (`buildDozer/Loader/Excavator/Hauler` keep the tool positions the gameplay uses: blade edge z 2.75, loader arm pivot and bucket tip, excavator boom 4.6 + stick 3.3, hauler bed hinge (0,1.62,-4.3)), `buildTrack` (instanced shoes), `Ram` (hydraulic rams), `machineMats` (per-machine grime shader, `U.uGround/uGrime`), trees/rocks/grass geometry.
- `crew.js`: the crew (Menu → Crew, `groundwork.crew`). `makeCrew(G)` gets what it needs from game.js; each worker returns the same control object the player makes (`m.ai`), and `update()` in game.js drives every machine with `m.ctl` (player input, `m.ai` or `ZERO`). With no job the crew follows the player (`convoy`, slots behind the player; `travel` uses A* `findPath` on a 2 m grid that avoids water deeper than each machine's ford and steep grades). Stopping at deep water makes a site with `siteAt` (aimed at the water's centroid, narrowest clear line). Sites are lines across the water (`s` along, `o` across); dozer/excavator/hauler roles with a lock so only one uses the line. `follow` = drive along the site line; `convoy` = follow the player (keep the names distinct). The player's machine has no AI; toasts from machines go through `say(m, msg)` (player's machine only).
- Soil you see (round 18): `soil(x, y, z, n, gravel, o)` emits small irregular pieces (`soilIM`, ring buffer `SMAX` by quality) that fall, bounce, roll down slopes steeper than ~30 degrees and settle; settled pieces vanish when the ground rises over them (buried) or after a while. Buckets and beds pour a stream off the lip (`m.pourT`, `m.pourV`, `m.pourFloor` for a hauler's bed); `fines` particles fall with it. The ground takes the soil when the stream lands: `dropCircle(..., defer, wait)` / `pourQ` `wait` = `fallTime(h)`. `chunks()` and `crumbs()` are thin wrappers. Material edges: `cellLook` blurs the raw grass/dirt/gravel mix (`cRaw`, 5x5 `BLUR5`) and pushes the grass line in and out with noise; ground only driven over (`D` set, `M` still 0, height unchanged) is worn turf with thin grass; the terrain shader breaks the edge up with `tMacro`.
- Crew driving (round 18): `aiHands` in game.js eases crew steering and throttle (and stops between forward and reverse); crew `goto` turns on the move and only pivots tracked machines for sharp turns; `findPath` routes are straightened by line of sight; `travel` slows for bends; the convoy follows the player's own track (`trail`, `TRAIL` distances) instead of fixed slots. `sitePen`: the driven machine can't move further into an unfinished bridge deck (`crew.roads`, `BRIDGE NOT FINISHED`).
- Land shape (round 18): `landH(x, z, d)` (inside the map and in `farH`); `GEN` 1 = the old gentle valley, kept for saves without `gen`; 2 = hilly/open regions, ridges, knolls (`knollAt`, stony `M = 2` tops), hollows, small relief. Saved as `gen`. Never change a version's formula (old saves' `H0` is rebuilt from it); add a new version instead.
- Trees (round 18): `spruceGeo(seed, h, detail)` = whorls of two crossed branch cards per branch (sag, lifted tip), a thin core, a spire; `leafyGeo(seed, h, detail)` = leaf clusters in lumps on limbs. Detail by quality (`TD` in game.js).
- Dirt: `H0` is the ground before digging (rebuilt from the seed on load); `SWELL` bulks never-dug soil; `dropCircle` tips a cone; the terrain shader gets `aDig` (H0, spoil depth) for soil layers. Erosion in `waterStep` only touches cells where `|H - H0| > .05` and not gravel.
- Trees: fallen trees stay (`st` 2, saved in `logs`), `pushLogs` shoves them; `treeFade` dithers trees in the line of sight (`aFade`, own materials, not the far forest's).
- `textures.js`: procedural textures (`makeTextures(quality)`), swapped for photos from `assets/` when they load (grass, dirt, gravel, bark, rock, `ground_h.png` = the three height maps in R/G/B; all CC0 from Poly Haven). `assets/sky.hdr` (Poly Haven, CC0) lights and reflects in the machines (`scene.environment`). `assets/foliage.png` (tree branches, spruce cards, leaf clumps; same layout as the old drawn atlas) is built by `tools/foliage.py` from Poly Haven's fir_tree_01 twig and tree_small_02 leaf photos. `assets/bark.jpg` + `bark_n.jpg` (Poly Haven chinese_cedar_bark, colour and bumps). Grass and gravel photos are colour-corrected to natural albedo (the originals are much brighter). Copies made with `clone()` of a swapped texture get the photo too (`photos()` in textures.js). `assets/wear.jpg` (R = paint tone, G = shine, from Poly Haven green_metal_rust) is projected from three sides onto every machine in `addGrime` (`tWear`). Boulders: `assets/rocks.bin` (seven scanned rocks from Poly Haven rock_moss_set_02, simplified to 1000 triangles each by `tools/rocks.mjs`, read by `parseRocks` in models.js) with `boulder.jpg`/`boulder_n.jpg` (glTF UVs: `flipY` false); one InstancedMesh per shape (`rockIMs`, each rock has `rk.im`/`rk.slot`); the drawn `rockGeo` stays until the file loads and for the crossing rock armour. Grass: `assets/tufts.png` (built by `tools/tufts.py` from Poly Haven grass_medium_01/02 and grass_bermuda_01, colour-matched to `grass.jpg`): eight short cells on top for meadow tufts, eight tall cells below for the water's edge; `tuftGeo` is three crossed cards and the grass shader picks the cell from the tuft's position (tall when the instance is stretched upwards). Keep assets phone-sized (1k or less).; `audio.js`: `Sound` (per-machine voices via `machine(id,cfg)`/`updateMachine`, one-shots, ambient). Sound values are guarded against NaN; keep that.
- Wild plants (round 17): `assets/plants.png` (built by `tools/plants.py` from Poly Haven fern_02, dandelion_01, celandine_01, shrub_03, shrub_sorrel_01, nettle_plant, weed_plant_02 parts; eight 256x256 cells: ferns, ferns, dandelions, celandine, wood sorrel, bush, nettles, dock). `fillPlants` (run with the grass refresh) places them from cell hashes, never random draws: ferns where `shadeMap` (standing trees within 6 m) is high, bushes at the forest edge, flower patches by noise in open meadow; plus `pebIMs` (three scanned rock shapes, small) on banks, shallows and bare ground. Turf hummocks: `hum(x, z)` in `hDraw` (only drawn; grass and plants add it to their height).
- Landscaping (round 17): `landscape.js` (`makeLandscape`), boss order LANDSCAPE (crew on). Three groundskeepers (people.js `kit`: addPerson/walk/animate/hold/face/standY) rake an 8 m circle (soil moves between neighbour cells, volume kept), seed it (`seedCell`, `SEED` growth + `seedH` height; `growSeed` turns it back to grass in ~90 s, cancelled if the height changes or it floods) and plant saplings (real trees with `planted: 1`, `g` growing .14 to 1 over 300 s), bushes and flowers (`garden`, drawn by `fillPlants`). Saved as `planted` (appended to `trees` on load before `gone`/`logs` are applied, so indices hold), `garden`, `seeded`, `land`.
- Graphics quality `groundwork.quality` (high/medium/low, `QS` in game.js). Tree count is capped by truncating the generation list (keeps old saves' `gone` indices valid); never add random draws to world generation, it would move trees/rocks in existing saves.
- `tests/play.cjs`: Playwright gameplay checks (`node tests/play.cjs [outdir]`). Run them after any gameplay change. If jsDelivr is unreachable, `npm install three@0.160.0` somewhere and set `THREE_DIR` to its `node_modules/three`.
