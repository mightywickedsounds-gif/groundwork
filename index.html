Jobs pay when the ground in the marked area is right. Level: flat at the see-through plane. Pond: dug down to the plane. Stockpile: gravel dumped inside the posts. The grey hill is the gravel pit.

Dirt slides if you pile it too steep, and wet banks slump sooner. Water runs downhill: cut a channel from the river and it follows; dam it and a lake builds up behind.
